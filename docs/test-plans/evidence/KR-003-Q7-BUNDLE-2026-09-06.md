## Evidence model and independence boundary

For every one of 100 fresh revisions, a separate no-permission ordinary fixture first proves the real ADB tap path by incrementing its own counter.
After expiry, 20 identical input-layer taps are injected across at least ten seconds. A valid automated row requires zero new fixture interactions,
zero focus gains, stable fixture instance/coordinate, a still-resumed underlying fixture, and unchanged candidate revision/service/permission/configuration
state. Candidate Accessibility telemetry supports timing and lifecycle checks but cannot make the independent fixture oracle pass.

This architecture is independent at the decision/output boundary: the fixture has a different package and UID, no Accessibility service, shared
storage, candidate callback, network permission or enforcement logic. Its own Android focus callbacks and button counter observe whether OS input
reaches the ordinary surface. The sender-protected debug receiver exports only numeric/boolean lab state and is absent from release.

The per-sample human requirement is eliminated **conditionally**, not by documentation alone. Before sample 1, checkpoint 1 must visibly agree with
automated denial and checkpoint 2 must show the same injected tap reaches the fixture after lab CLEAR. Failure or uncertainty stops the run and
leaves the historical 100-human Q6 requirement incompatible with the owner's three-checkpoint limit pending explicit go/no-go/product direction.

## Exact owner workflow

With only the authorized Mi 8 connected, start one owner-operated Windows PowerShell terminal:

```powershell
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\platform-tools\kr003-qualification-bundles\4b886e4\Start-KR003.ps1" -OfflineNetwork
```

The three human checkpoint sessions are:

1. Before sample 1, observe one excluded restricted expiry for ten seconds and report whether it stays continuously visible, denies ordinary use and does not flicker.
2. Immediately after the runner's lab-only CLEAR and injected tap, report whether the overlay is absent and the ordinary fixture visibly responds. Automation independently requires exactly one counter increment.
3. After unattended sample 100, observe the held restriction, then complete the guided physical Home, root Settings, expected Digital Wellbeing block, one-button persistent Settings recovery and ordinary re-entry checks. The final CLEAR/input control is automated.

Keep the terminal open, the device unlocked/interactive and available at those prompts. Samples 1–100 require no keypress or observation and take
approximately 35–45 minutes unattended. Any input leakage, focus regain, missing block, stale revision, process/service/configuration change, metric
mismatch, physical F/I/Q or final p95 above 2,000 ms stops and preserves evidence. There is no row replacement, resume or result pooling.

The runner journals original radio flags, disables Wi-Fi/mobile data, requires offline confirmation, and independently attempts sample-preserving
lab CLEAR plus radio restoration/readback during finalization. Radio flags are not proof of end-to-end connectivity.
