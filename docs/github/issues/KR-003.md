# KR-003 — Prove or reject consumer Android enforcement and Play feasibility

## Goal

Resolve the highest-risk architecture assumption before production enforcement.

## Context

ADR-0002 compares consumer Accessibility, Device Admin, Device Owner and Lock Task. The targeted overlay fix and ten-cycle Mi 8 checkpoint have
owner-observed evidence; 100-sample qualification, remaining device/safety/lifecycle matrix and current Android/Play policy tension remain open.
The d81f19a calibration adds one observed expiry and physical Home/Settings success, but its recovery oracle was uncorroborated and reporting failed.
Q2 repairs/diagnostics require calibration-only verification before the 100-sample run; Android-side cause remains UNSPECIFIED.
That Q2 verification has now **physically failed Settings/recovery** despite a successful expiry and Home resistance. Current candidate cannot
qualify; exact OEM/task cause is UNSPECIFIED. Preserve this physical FAIL separately from the earlier oracle-only disagreement.
Q3 then physically recorded Settings-root PASS, Digital Wellbeing/recovery FAIL and phase-local ORDINARY_APP reattachment. The automatic debug
CLEAR preserved latency samples. Q4 then physically rejected the bounded `NEW_TASK | CLEAR_TOP` candidate: Settings flashed transiently before
`ORDINARY_APP` returned and enforcement reattached. Q5 is the final bounded flag-only task-reset candidate; its physical result is
**UNSPECIFIED** and qualification remains blocked.

## Scope

Use an isolated minimal native spike with no content inspection. Verify declared permission/runtime path, offline expiry, normal process/reboot recovery, safe system access, OEM limits and policy disclosure. Record go/no-go with support boundary.

## Out of scope

Production enforcement, stealth, uninstall interception, device-owner provisioning as ordinary pairing, root bypass, broad monitoring or final store submission.

- [ ] AC-2: Official current Accessibility guide vs Play policy tension is explicitly assessed; a concrete disclosure/refusal/declaration packet exists, with approval status UNSPECIFIED until obtained.

- [ ] AC-6: No content/node/text history is read/retained/uploaded; merged permissions and test traffic confirm least privilege.
- [ ] AC-7: Owner accepts a tested support boundary or chooses a scope change; no production go-ahead is inferred from a document.
