# KR-003 — Prove or reject consumer Android enforcement and Play feasibility

## Goal

Resolve the highest-risk architecture assumption before production enforcement.

## Context

ADR-0002 compares consumer Accessibility, Device Admin, Device Owner and Lock Task. The targeted overlay fix and ten-cycle Mi 8 checkpoint have
owner-observed evidence; 100-sample qualification, remaining device/safety/lifecycle matrix and current Android/Play policy tension remain open.
Q2/Q3 physically failed Settings recovery, and Q4 rejected the bounded `NEW_TASK | CLEAR_TOP` candidate when Settings flashed transiently before
`ORDINARY_APP` returned. The final flag-only Q5 `NEW_TASK | CLEAR_TASK` candidate then physically passed that exact focused route once: root PASS,
expected Digital Wellbeing block, persistent recovery PASS and verified lab CLEAR. Q5 has zero qualification rows. This permits preparation of a
new immutable 100-sample runner using the exact APK; it does not satisfy AC-3, broader safe surfaces/devices/lifecycle checks or Play approval.
Q6 was packaged but never run. OD-29 supersedes it with Q7: mandatory offline execution, 100 unattended active fixture-oracle cycles and at most
three human checkpoint sessions. Q7 must physically calibrate the oracle before sample 1 and cannot be reported as 100 human-visible passes.

Three Q7 attempts stopped before ARM/sample 1. Subsequent [transport and owner configuration evidence](../../test-plans/evidence/KR-003-MI8-INPUT-DENIAL-2026-09-06.md)
confirm shell INPUT_TAP exit 1 / SECURITY_EXCEPTION and disabled SIM-gated security debugging. The subsequent
[UiAutomation preflight](../../test-plans/evidence/KR-003-UIAUTOMATION-DENIAL-2026-09-06.md) also denied DOWN injection; framework finish returned and fixture counter stayed zero.
The [bounded Monkey run](../../test-plans/evidence/KR-003-MONKEY-DENIAL-2026-09-07.md) also denied DOWN injection, with zero taps and verified cleanup.
Q7 is explicitly blocked on the unchanged Mi 8: no tested transport satisfies its positive control there. The visual fallback conflicts with the maximum-three-human constraint.
OD-31's generic path has now produced one fixture-only transport PASS on the exact Samsung SM-X400 / Android 16 API 36 / build
`BP4A.251205.006` configuration. Three source-`5a46f75` oracle calibrations remain INVALID before positive control/ARM because runner Accessibility
verification reported not granted while candidate Accessibility/heartbeat/health were healthy. The fresh runner-v2 calibration then passed Usage,
Accessibility, heartbeat, candidate health and the independent positive control, but stopped `INVALID:HOST_EXCEPTION` during ARM before attachment,
blocked hold or owner prompt. The v2 exception type/message was not retained and is **UNSPECIFIED**. The runner-v3 invocation failed on a reserved
PowerShell `$script:Host` assignment before output creation or any device command. Runner v4 minimally renames internal state and adds static and
real-entrypoint cross-version regressions; its replacement bundle is not yet physically run. All gates remain intact; Open/In Progress administration
and draft PR #16 are preserved. No 100 samples exist and KR-004 is untouched.
